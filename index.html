🏭 State-of-the-Art Farsan Manufacturing

Where Traditional Halwai Mastery Meets Modern Food Science

At Swadangan Foods, every batch of our BASURI range is crafted using automated continuous fryers, optical lentil sorters, and touch-free spice tumbling drums—preserving authentic home-style crunch with export-grade hygiene.

🌾 100% Pure Besan & Pulses Zero adulteration; stone-ground chana, moong, and moth dal sourced directly from Rajasthan & Gujarat mandis.
🛡️ Nitrogen-Flushed 3-Layer Packs Locks in factory-fresh aroma, prevents oil oxidation, and guarantees a 6-month crispy shelf life.
01

Optical Grain Selection

Laser color-sorted chana dal, yellow moong dal, and bold Saurashtra groundnuts ensure uniform size and zero impurities.

02

In-House Spice Grinding

Whole Guntur chillies, Ratlami cloves, black pepper, and dry mango are cryo-ground in-house to retain natural essential oils.

03

Precision Batch Frying

Temperature-controlled continuous frying in fresh, zero-trans-fat vegetable oil delivers feather-light crunch without greasiness.

04

Nitrogen-Flushed Sealing

Immediate multi-head weighing into royal 400g & 500g metallized pouches with food-grade nitrogen flushing.
